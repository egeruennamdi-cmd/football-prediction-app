User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: October 2026 • Meta Platform Compliance & Data Protection Standard

At DeepPredictBet, we respect your privacy and uphold strict data minimization, digital sovereignty, and transparency principles. In accordance with Meta Platform Terms, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), this page provides clear, actionable instructions on how users and administrators can delete personal data associated with DeepPredictBet and its Facebook/Meta integrations.

📌 Key Overview for Facebook Users & Administrators:
DeepPredictBet integrates with the Meta Graph API solely to allow authorized team administrators to connect and publish automated match previews and statistical updates to the official DeepPredictBet Facebook Page. DeepPredictBet does not offer a "Log in with Facebook" mechanism to regular website visitors or punters. We do not access or store personal Facebook profiles, friends lists, private messages, or external user activities.

1. Data Stored in Connection with Facebook/Meta

When an authorized administrator connects the official Facebook Page via our Social Media Command Center, DeepPredictBet stores only the bare technical credentials required to communicate with the Meta Graph API:

  • Page Access Token: An encrypted OAuth token used by server-side Cloudflare Pages Functions to publish posts on behalf of the Page.
  • Page Identifiers: The public Facebook Page ID and Page Name (e.g., "DeepPredictBet Official").
  • Scope Metadata: The specific permissions authorized during OAuth (pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, pages_show_list).
  • Publication Logs: External post IDs returned by Meta upon successful publication (e.g., fb_post_12345) to confirm delivery and avoid duplicate posts.

All access tokens and connection data are stored exclusively in encrypted server-side Cloudflare KV storage (USERS_KV) and are never exposed to client browsers or third parties.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

Depending on your relationship with the platform, you may delete your data through any of the following verified methods:

Method A: Remove DeepPredictBet from Your Facebook Account

If you connected a Facebook Page or authorized the DeepPredictBet application, you can revoke access directly through Facebook at any time:

1
Log into your Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
2
In the left sidebar, click Apps and Websites (or go directly to facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).
3
Locate DeepPredictBet Publisher (or the associated app name).
4
Click Remove and confirm. This immediately invalidates the active Page Access Token and prevents DeepPredictBet from making any future requests to the Meta Graph API on your behalf.
⚠️ Important Notice on Disconnecting vs. Deleting:
Removing the app in your Facebook settings revokes Meta's authorization, but it does not automatically erase existing cached tokens or account records previously stored on DeepPredictBet's servers. To purge all server-side records, use Method B or Method C below.

Method B: Instant Disconnect via the Command Center (Administrators)

Authorized administrators can immediately purge Facebook connection data from our Cloudflare KV storage:

1
Log in to the DeepPredictBet administrator panel and open the Social Media & Telegram Command Center.
2
Select the Facebook Pages channel.
3
Click Disconnect Account and confirm. Our server executes an immediate purge of the social_connection_facebook KV entry, deleting all tokens, page identifiers, and verification records permanently.

Method C: Submit a Written Deletion Request via Email

If you are a registered platform member or an administrator who wishes to request manual data erasure, please contact our verified Data Protection desk:

  • Contact Email: privacy@deeppredictbet.com
  • Alternative Support Desk: support@deeppredictbet.com
  • Subject Line: User Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / Page / Account]
  • Required Information:
    • Your registered DeepPredictBet email address or username (if applicable).
    • The Facebook Page Name or Page ID associated with the integration.
    • A clear statement requesting permanent erasure of your stored data.

3. What Information Is Deleted vs. Retained

When a data deletion request is processed:

  • Data Permanently Deleted:
    • All stored Meta OAuth tokens, Page Access Tokens, and account mappings.
    • Stored user profile data, login credentials, and session records.
    • Stored preferences, notification settings, and bet builder drafts.
  • Data Retained for Legitimate Legal or Security Reasons:
    • Financial & Tax Compliance: If you purchased a VIP subscription, payment transaction receipts and invoice records may be retained for statutory periods required by tax and accounting regulations.
    • Security & Abuse Defense: Ephemeral network connection logs maintained by Cloudflare (such as IP addresses associated with malicious DDoS or scraping attacks) are retained strictly for infrastructure protection in accordance with legitimate interest provisions.

4. Confirmation & Processing Timeframe

  • Acknowledgement: Our Data Protection Officer acknowledges all email deletion requests within 48 business hours of receipt.
  • Processing Period: Under standard data protection regulations (GDPR Article 12(3) and CCPA), verified deletion requests are fully executed within thirty (30) calendar days.
  • Written Confirmation: Once your records have been permanently expunged from production datastores and server backups, you will receive a final confirmation email containing a formal closure statement.
