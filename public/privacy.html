Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 3.4 • Strict Anti-Surveillance Standard

At DeepPredictBet, user privacy, pseudonymity, and data minimization are foundational engineering pillars. We design our sports prediction platform to respect your right to private, unmonitored sports analytics. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we process, how we safeguard your device interactions, and our strict commitment to zero surveillance.

🔒 The DeepPredictBet Privacy Pledge:
  • Zero Bet Slip Surveillance: We never monitor, store, or sell your private betting slips or converted booking codes.
  • Zero Financial Data Retention: We never hold credit cards, banking pins, or payment account numbers on our servers.
  • Zero Ad-Tracking Cookies: We do not sell user profiles to third-party ad brokers or predatory gambling syndicates.

1. Information We Collect (Data Minimization)

We collect only the bare technical minimum required to operate and secure the platform:

  • Account Identifiers: If you register, we store an email address, encrypted authentication credential hash (via PBKDF2/Argon2), and pseudonym username.
  • Product Telemetry: Anonymized interaction events (e.g. tool feature clicks, algorithmic model compute latency, converter success rates) logged with ephemeral session identifiers to audit platform stability.
  • Local Storage Assets: Watchlists, customized date presets, and bet builder drafts are stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage and are never transmitted to marketing trackers.

2. How We Use Information

Any collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating member access and maintaining active subscription entitlements.
  • Preventing distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, automated scraping abuse, and session hijacking.
  • Evaluating the algorithmic accuracy and calibration of our mathematical prediction models against settled fixtures.
  • Delivering customer support responses to inquiries submitted through the Help Console.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Bookmaker Links

DeepPredictBet provides booking code translation across multiple external sportsbooks (such as SportyBet, Bet9ja, 1xBet, BetKing, and others). When you click an external link or copy a converted booking code, you interact directly with that third-party bookmaker according to their respective privacy policies and licensing jurisdictions. DeepPredictBet does not transmit your personal account credentials to any external sportsbook.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, we extend full digital data sovereignty:

  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated session history at any time through the Settings console or by emailing support.
  • Right to Export: You can export your stored settings and activity logs directly from the platform as a portable JSON file.

5. Cookie Policy & Security Controls

DeepPredictBet uses strictly necessary HTTP-only authentication session cookies and Cloudflare edge verification tokens. We do not use third-party behavioral tracking cookies, cross-site pixels, or retargeting beacons.

6. Privacy Inquiries & Data Protection Officer

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our security infrastructure, please contact our Data Protection desk at privacy@deeppredictbet.com.
