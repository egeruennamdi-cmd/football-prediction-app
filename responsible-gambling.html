Responsible Gambling Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • 18+ Mandatory • Global Harm Reduction Standard
🛡️ 18+ Only | Bet Responsibly: Betting involves financial risk and the potential to lose capital. DeepPredictBet exists to equip sports fans with transparent mathematical intelligence so they make informed decisions and avoid reckless wagering. Never gamble with money you need for rent, bills, or essentials.

1. Core Responsible Gaming Principles

We encourage all visitors to adhere to the following golden rules:

  • Set Strict Limits: Decide your entertainment bankroll beforehand and never exceed it under any circumstances.
  • Never Chase Losses: Increasing stakes after a loss is the fastest path to problem gambling. Accept losses as the cost of sports entertainment.
  • Treat Sports as Recreation: Sports betting is NOT an investment, career path, or guaranteed method to earn income.
  • Maintain Perspective: Never wager under the influence of alcohol, emotional distress, or financial duress.

2. How DeepPredictBet Protects Bettors

Unlike predatory tipster services that advertise “100% Fixed Matches” or “Guaranteed Wins,” DeepPredictBet enforces strict protective measures across our software:

  • Transparent Probabilities: We prominently display mathematical variance, Poisson goal distributions, and true implied odds on all predictions.
  • Bet Doctor Accumulator Health Audits: Our Bet Doctor highlights correlation traps and high-risk accumulator legs to discourage reckless slips.
  • Zero Deceptive Marketing: We never promote misleading claims, predatory guarantees, or get-rich-quick narratives.

3. Self-Assessment: Do You Have a Gambling Problem?

Ask yourself these questions honestly:

  • Do you wager more than you can comfortably afford to lose?
  • Do you borrow money or sell possessions to finance betting?
  • Has your betting caused stress, anxiety, or arguments with family or friends?
  • Do you feel an irresistible urge to immediately win back lost funds?
  • Have you lied to others about the amount of time or money you spend betting?

If you answered “yes” to any of these questions, we strongly urge you to take a break and contact an accredited support organization immediately.

4. Free, Confidential Problem Gambling Helplines

  • United Kingdom: BeGambleAware (begambleaware.org) • Freephone 24/7 Helpline: 0808 8020 133
  • GamCare (UK): Free live chat and telephone support at gamcare.org.uk
  • United States: National Problem Gambling Helpline: 1-800-522-4700 (Call or Text 24/7) • ncpgambling.org
  • International: Gambling Therapy (gamblingtherapy.org) — Multi-lingual support and live peer forums.
